# Calculus I Trig Identity Drill

A single-page browser app for memorizing and practicing the trigonometric identities commonly used in **Calculus I**.

<img width="629" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/29313c3d-a4ce-4f4f-a1ac-8c3252056ce0" />

Link to live app: https://realityexpander.github.io/Calculus-I-Trig-Identities-Drills-for-Cengage-WebAssign/

Instead of multiple-choice questions, the app requires the user to **write the missing side of each identity in an equation editor**. Questions can be drilled left-to-right, right-to-left, or in both directions randomly.

The app displays mathematical expressions in LaTeX-style notation and uses an interactive math input field for entering answers.

## Features

- Written-answer trig identity practice — **no multiple choice**
- Interactive equation entry using [MathLive](https://mathlive.io/)
- Symbolic answer parsing using the [CortexJS Compute Engine](https://cortexjs.io/compute-engine/)
- Drill one identity section at a time or all sections together
- Practice identities in:
  - left-to-right direction
  - right-to-left direction
  - both directions randomly
- Questions are randomly selected
- Avoids immediately repeating the same question when possible
- Displays the LaTeX generated by the equation editor
- **Check Answer** button
- **Reveal** button
- **Next Identity** button
- Score tracking:
  - Completed
  - Correct
  - Current streak
- **Reset Score** button
- Responsive layout for desktop and mobile browsers
- Runs from a single HTML file

## Identity Sections

The drill is divided into the following ten sections.

### 1. Reciprocal Identities

$$
\csc x=\frac{1}{\sin x}
$$

$$
\sec x=\frac{1}{\cos x}
$$

$$
\cot x=\frac{1}{\tan x}
$$

### 2. Quotient Identities

$$
\tan x=\frac{\sin x}{\cos x}
$$

$$
\cot x=\frac{\cos x}{\sin x}
$$

### 3. Pythagorean Identities

$$
\sin^2x+\cos^2x=1
$$

$$
1+\tan^2x=\sec^2x
$$

$$
1+\cot^2x=\csc^2x
$$

### 4. Useful Rearrangements

$$
1-\sin^2x=\cos^2x
$$

$$
1-\cos^2x=\sin^2x
$$

$$
\sec^2x-1=\tan^2x
$$

$$
\csc^2x-1=\cot^2x
$$

$$
1-\sec^2x=-\tan^2x
$$

$$
1-\csc^2x=-\cot^2x
$$

### 5. Even / Odd Identities

$$
\sin(-x)=-\sin x
$$

$$
\cos(-x)=\cos x
$$

$$
\tan(-x)=-\tan x
$$

$$
\csc(-x)=-\csc x
$$

$$
\sec(-x)=\sec x
$$

$$
\cot(-x)=-\cot x
$$

### 6. Double-Angle Identities

$$
\sin(2x)=2\sin x\cos x
$$

$$
\cos(2x)=\cos^2x-\sin^2x
$$

$$
\cos(2x)=2\cos^2x-1
$$

$$
\cos(2x)=1-2\sin^2x
$$

$$
\tan(2x)=\frac{2\tan x}{1-\tan^2x}
$$

### 7. Power-Reduction / Half-Angle Forms

$$
\sin^2x=\frac{1-\cos(2x)}{2}
$$

$$
\cos^2x=\frac{1+\cos(2x)}{2}
$$

### 8. Sum and Difference Identities

$$
\sin(a+b)=\sin a\cos b+\cos a\sin b
$$

$$
\sin(a-b)=\sin a\cos b-\cos a\sin b
$$

$$
\cos(a+b)=\cos a\cos b-\sin a\sin b
$$

$$
\cos(a-b)=\cos a\cos b+\sin a\sin b
$$

$$
\tan(a+b)=\frac{\tan a+\tan b}{1-\tan a\tan b}
$$

$$
\tan(a-b)=\frac{\tan a-\tan b}{1+\tan a\tan b}
$$

### 9. Cofunction Identities

$$
\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{2}-x\right)=\cos x
$$

$$
\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{2}-x\right)=\sin x
$$

$$
\tan\left(\frac{\pi}{2}-x\right)=\cot x
$$

$$
\sec\left(\frac{\pi}{2}-x\right)=\csc x
$$

### 10. Core Seven — Highest Priority Review

This section repeats seven high-priority identities for focused review:

$$
\tan x=\frac{\sin x}{\cos x}
$$

$$
\cot x=\frac{\cos x}{\sin x}
$$

$$
\sec x=\frac{1}{\cos x}
$$

$$
\csc x=\frac{1}{\sin x}
$$

$$
\sin^2x+\cos^2x=1
$$

$$
1+\tan^2x=\sec^2x
$$

$$
1+\cot^2x=\csc^2x
$$

## How the Drill Works

Each question displays one side of an identity and leaves the other side blank.

For example:

$$
\tan x=\boxed{\phantom{\frac{\sin x}{\cos x}}}
$$

The user enters:

$$
\frac{\sin x}{\cos x}
$$

The direction can also be reversed:

$$
\boxed{\phantom{\tan x}}=\frac{\sin x}{\cos x}
$$

The user then enters:

$$
\tan x
$$

When **Both directions (random)** is selected, the app randomly decides which side of the identity is shown.

## Running the App

No installation or build process is required.

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open `Calculus_I_trig_identity_drill.html` in a modern web browser.
3. Select a section and drill direction.
4. Type the missing expression into the equation field.
5. Press **Check Answer**.

Pressing **Enter** while editing the answer also checks the current response.

## Internet Requirement

The app itself is contained in a single HTML file, but it currently loads its math libraries from a CDN:

- [MathLive](https://mathlive.io/)
- [CortexJS Compute Engine](https://cortexjs.io/compute-engine/)

Because of this, an internet connection is required when the page starts.

## Answer Checking

The app first attempts to compare the entered expression with the expected expression using the CortexJS Compute Engine.

If symbolic comparison is not available for a particular expression, the app falls back to normalized LaTeX comparison.

This allows normal equation-editor input while still checking the expected memorized identity form.

## Score Tracking

The app tracks three values during the current session:

| Statistic | Description |
|---|---|
| **Completed** | Number of identities answered correctly or revealed |
| **Correct** | Number answered correctly without using Reveal |
| **Current streak** | Consecutive correct answers |

Selecting **Reset Score** clears all three values and starts a new question.

Scores are session-only and are not currently saved when the browser is closed or refreshed.

## Purpose

This app is intended as a study aid for students who need to **recall trigonometric identities from memory**, especially in Calculus I where these identities frequently appear in:

- differentiation
- implicit differentiation
- limits
- algebraic simplification
- integration preparation
- trigonometric substitutions and manipulations

The emphasis is on **active recall** rather than recognizing the correct answer from a list of choices.
